---
name: getStartSlots slot alignment gotcha
description: Slots from getStartSlots are always aligned to 30-min multiples from midnight, so durations like 45 min produce last slots that are multiples of 30, not of the duration itself.
type: feedback
---

`getStartSlots` increments by `SLOT_INTERVAL_MINUTES` (30 min), not by the duration. This means that even with `duration=45`, generated slot times are always multiples of 30 minutes from midnight (e.g., 09:00, 09:30, …, 17:00). The last slot for duration 45 is 17:00 (not 17:15), because 17:15 is never generated by the 30-min step.

**Why:** Discovered when a test asserted 17:15 as the last slot for duration 45 and failed — the actual last slot was 17:00.

**How to apply:** When writing tests for `getStartSlots`, always derive expected last slots by finding the largest multiple of 30 (from clinic start) that satisfies `min + duration <= clinicEnd`, not by subtracting the duration from clinicEnd directly.
